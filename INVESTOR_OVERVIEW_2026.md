# AliceLabs — Product and business overview

Updated: 17 November 2026.

AliceLabs LLC is a Wyoming software company built by Edison Flores from Quito, Ecuador. This page provides public product context for prospective commercial partners and investment reviewers. Financial information and proposed financing terms are shared separately on request.

## Primary commercial focus: UTA Shield / MarketNow

**Customer:** operators publishing HTTP Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers.

**Problem:** publishing a tool interface requires controls over request identity, per-tool permissions, sensitive output and operational records.

**Current implementation:** the UTA Shield source documents shared-secret request authentication, per-tool access controls, rate limiting, DLP checks and JSONL audit records. MarketNow provides related discovery and trust tooling. Catalog entries and technical checks must not be interpreted as paying users or independent certification.

**Commercial offer:** a scoped assessment and deployment pilot, with the published starting price of US$1,000 setup plus optional US$100/month/server support. Scope, compatibility, acceptance criteria and service terms are agreed before a paid engagement.

## Commercial validation plan

[Detailed commercial validation plan](COMMERCIAL_PLAN_UTA_SHIELD_2026.md), including pilot scope, proposed unit economics and 90-day milestones.

1. Agree a narrow paid pilot with a defined operator and acceptance criteria.
2. Record deployment effort, support cost and customer feedback.
3. Establish which customers choose ongoing support.
4. Expand the repeatable offer only after that evidence exists.

Other repositories include supporting packages, experiments, mirrors, operational tooling and third-party forks. Repository count is not presented as customer traction or an estimate of enterprise value.
